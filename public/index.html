A tiny studio. Anywhere.

Tap a few pads at once or use 1234 / QWER / ASDF / ZXCV. Use the arrows to cycle through seven instruments. Choose a grid from 2×2 to 8×8. On compact screens, swipe larger grids sideways. In Edit pads, tap any pad to assign another instrument or sound.

Catch a happy accident.

Press Record, play up to 30 seconds, then press Record again. Loop your take or save it as a WAV. Volume, pitch, and room settings are captured with each hit. Settings lets you choose a root note, scale, and octave for pitched instruments.

Keep it in your pocket.

On your iPad, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. After the offline indicator says ready, the sounds work without a connection. Enable audio plays a check tone; tap Test audio to hear it again. If it’s silent, raise the iPad’s media volume and check its Bluetooth output.
