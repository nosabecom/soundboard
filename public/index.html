01 · Pads, Chords & Rolls

Tap pads, glide across them to strum, or play with 1234 / QWER / ASDF / ZXCV. Cycle nine instruments—including 808 & Bass and Lush pad. On pitched instruments, choose 7th Lush or 9th Jazz in Chord mode to trigger full Neo-Soul voicings from single pads. Set ROLL (or hold Shift) to perform tempo-locked hi-hat and snare rolls.

02 · Sequencer & Overdub Looper

Press ▷ Run (or Space) to start the 16-step groovebox—featuring Lagos Bounce, 90s Boom Bap, UK 2-Step, and more. Tap any pad to view its track and toggle steps 1–16. Press Record to capture a live loop, then switch instruments and press Overdub to layer basslines and chords with ↶ Undo.

03 · Live FX, Share & Offline

Sweep the DJ Filter left for warm low-pass or right for high-pass drops, dial in stereo Echo and Room reverb, and export everything—including sequencer bars and tails—via Save WAV. Use ↗ Share to copy a link to your custom board and beat. On iPad, Share → Add to Home Screen for full offline play.
